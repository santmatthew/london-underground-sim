Write a simulation of the experience of riding the London underground. For now we do not need the exterior / above ground we just need to start at a random station - but it could be a random point within the station - so one needs to find their way around the station to the platform. The game mechanics are pretty simple - you get given a destination and you need to get there in the shortest time. A lot of factors can affect this:
- time of day - rush hour etc
- train timetable
- path taken within station
etc
The experience needs to feel realistic with great texture and people looking very real

Eventually the mechanics become multi stop and visiting n stations in shortest time.

Use Godot (/usr/bin/godot) and Blender
